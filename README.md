Deep Learning from Scratch

A hands-on journey through deep learning, from building neural networks from scratch to implementing modern architectures in PyTorch.

What’s inside:

Neural networks built from scratch, without frameworks, to understand the fundamentals
PyTorch implementations of key architectures: MLPs, CNNs, RNNs, autoencoders and VAEs
A mini GPT built step by step, from a character-level tokenizer to self-attention
My own small LLM, trained in a Jupyter notebook

Inspired by Andrej Karpathy’s lectures on building neural networks and language models from the ground up.
